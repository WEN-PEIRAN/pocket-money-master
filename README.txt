POCKET MONEY MASTER — VERSION 2.9

Mobile Wallet interaction
- Selecting a rear card moves it to the front.
- Selecting the front card opens its account.
- The card touched at pointer-down is retained through pointer capture.
- Any movement below the 45 px vertical swipe threshold is treated as a tap.
- The previous mobile no-action zone has been removed.
- A click fallback supports browsers with inconsistent pointer delivery.
- Swipe-generated clicks are temporarily suppressed to prevent accidental opening.
- The front account card displays Tap to open; the front Add Account card displays Tap to create.
